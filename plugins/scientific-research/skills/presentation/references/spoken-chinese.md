# Spoken Chinese for the script

Setting: a PhD student presenting to their professor (lab meeting, progress report, proposal, defense rehearsal). The script is in Traditional Chinese (zh-TW) and must sound like a person talking: spoken word choice, short sentences, but no filler. Precise and calm, neither casual nor stiff.

## Sentence shape

- One idea per sentence, about 25 characters or fewer per clause. Long sentences are hard to say and hard to follow.
- Break long modifier chains before a noun. 「一個基於注意力且能處理不定長序列的編碼器」 → 「這是一個用 attention 的編碼器，它可以處理長度不固定的序列。」
- Subject first, then the verb. Avoid passive 「被」 unless it is natural.
- Use 我們 for our own work. Address the professor as 老師 only when it helps (asking for input, pointing at a figure), not in every paragraph.

## English terms and numbers

- Keep technical terms people actually say in English: Transformer, attention, baseline, loss, fine-tune, dataset names, model names. Do not force awkward translations.
- Do not mix generic English words into Chinese: not 「這個 performance 很 significant」, but 「這個結果明顯比較好」.
- Read numbers the way they are said: 0.853 → 「85.3%」 or 「零點八五三」; 1e-4 → 「十的負四次方」; 32k → 「三萬兩千」.
- Explain an equation by what it does, not symbol by symbol: 「這一項是在懲罰兩個表示差太遠的情況」.

## Banned

- Particles and fillers: 啊、啦、喔、嘛、呢、吧、耶、欸、齁、嗯.
- Verbal tics: 「那個」、句首的「然後」、「就是說」、「基本上」、「其實」、「XX 的部分」, 「做一個 XX 的動作」, 「進行一個 XX」.
- Stage directions inside the script: 「這頁要強調」、「這裡停頓」、「可以說」. Turn them into lines the speaker actually says.
- Overclaiming: 「完全解決」「一定」. Say what was measured: 「在這三個資料集上都比 baseline 高」.

## Professional PhD tone

- State the claim, then the evidence, then the limit: 「目前看起來 X 有效，證據是 Y，但只測了 Z，還不能確定能推廣」.
- Separate result from interpretation: 「結果是…」 vs 「我們的推測是…」.
- Bring up weaknesses before the professor does, and say what the next step is.
- When unsure, say so plainly: 「這點我還沒確認，會再查」. No flattery, no apologizing for the slides.

## Patterns for common moments

- Transition (Püschel p.27): 「剛剛講的是 A，接下來看 B。」
- Presenting a plot (Püschel p.39): 「這張圖比較的是 A 和 B。橫軸是…，縱軸是…，越高越好。以這個點來看，…。整體來說，…。」
- Asking for a decision: 「這裡有兩個方向，A 和 B。我比較傾向 A，因為…。想請老師看哪一個比較合適。」
